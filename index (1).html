Goal-based financial advice for Mumbai professionals

Mumbai runs at its own pace, and so do its earners. Salaries here tend to be higher, but so do expenses, ambitions and the number of competing financial goals. Nidesh Financial Services works with professionals across the city who want a calm, structured plan rather than a stream of hot tips. We are an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor, advising over 200 families and more than ₹60 lakh in assets since 2016 from our base in the Kalyan–Thane belt, and we serve Mumbai clients both in person and remotely.

For a Mumbai professional, the challenge is rarely a lack of income — it is a lack of time and a clear framework. Money arrives, gets spent or parked, and years slip by without a plan tying it to what you actually want: a home, your children's education, financial independence. Our job is to build that framework with you and keep it on track.

How we help

  • Goal-based mutual fund planning — mapping each goal to a suitable investment strategy and timeline.
  • SIP planning — automating disciplined monthly investing that scales as your income grows.
  • ELSS tax saving — using tax-saving funds efficiently within your wider plan.
  • SWP retirement income — designing a withdrawal strategy that can pay you a regular income later.
  • Demat support — getting your accounts and paperwork in order with minimal fuss.

Who this suits

We work well with mid-career professionals juggling several goals, higher earners who want their surplus invested with intent, and busy people who simply want a trustworthy second opinion on what they already hold. Because we are a distributor and not a product factory, the focus stays on what is right for your plan.
